# Contributor conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, reviews and project
spaces. Focus criticism on the work. Welcome questions and corrections, give
credit, and protect personal and confidential information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content directed at people,
doxxing and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful content and restrict participation. Apply these decisions
consistently and explain them where doing so respects the people involved.

Report a concern to the repository owner through a private contact on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/mikevocalz), if available. Otherwise,
open a minimal issue requesting a private contact without naming affected
people or publishing sensitive details. Maintainers should handle reports
privately and avoid retaliation.
