---
title: Development client on the Pi 400
description: 'Six captures of the Metro development client on the Raspberry Pi 400: Fast Refresh, LogBox, the developer menu and the Metro-unreachable screen.'
---

Six captures from the 2026-11-13 development client run. The receipt says input was injected through Screen with `rnqnx-inject key`, because pointer injection did not reach the right coordinates under the window manager at that time. The counter was driven from the keyboard-navigable developer menu.

The receipt also lists a device log excerpt and the Metro terminal start. Both are text files next to the images.
