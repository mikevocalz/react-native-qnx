---
title: WebGPU on the Pi 400
description: 'Two captures of the Dawn WebGPU test app on the Raspberry Pi 400: a display screenshot and a Qt scene graph grab.'
---

Stage 5 of the v2 plan ran a Dawn WebGPU test on the board. The receipt says the app passed 10 of 10 stages and that no JavaScript API or Qt Quick item uses it yet. Only 640x480-class windows ran, and a fullscreen 1920x1080 surface was not tried.

The status of this work is on the [status page](/docs/reference/status).
